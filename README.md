Maya settlement. Great Pyramid. Gods that do not wait.  
A city-survival game in the spirit of **Frostpunk** — born from our GMTK jam prototype, rebuilt for a full campaign of hard choices.

You manage people, heat, faith, and scarce resources while the countdown to the End of the World never stops.  
Every edict has a cost. Every tribute buys time. Every delay kills someone.

> Keep the fires lit. Feed the gods. Decide who survives the descent.

## Why this exists

The jam version proved the fantasy: plaza, pyramid, tribute, no autopilot logistics.  
This repo is the long game — deeper systems, campaign structure, hope and discontent, laws, weather/crises, and a city that feels alive under the gods' shadow.

Prototype reference (read-only inspiration): sibling repo **gmtk_2026**.
